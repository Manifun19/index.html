Exptra Ai translates and explains what is on your phone's screen, in the place where it is, and lets you take, mark and share screenshots. This page says what the app reads, what leaves the phone, and what is kept.

Accessibility Service

Exptra Ai uses Android's Accessibility Service to work over other apps. It is not an accessibility tool for people with disabilities. You turn it on yourself, after the app shows you what it reads and does. With it, the app:

  • reads the text on the screen of the app in front, and where each piece is, to show its translation in the same place (password fields are never read);
  • takes a picture of the screen (a screenshot) when text cannot be read otherwise, to match the look of the page, when you circle something to explain it, or when you tap the screenshot bubble. It is kept in memory only and discarded after use, unless you save or share it yourself;
  • notes which app is in front, to stay out of bank, payment and password apps (nothing is captured or sent there);
  • passes your own taps, scrolls and long presses on to the app under it. It never taps, types or changes anything by itself.

You can turn it off at any time: phone Settings → Accessibility → Exptra Ai, or the bubble → Turn off.

What stays on your phone

  • Translation and reading of text happen on the phone (Google ML Kit's on-device models).
  • A cache of short translated words, which you can clear in Settings.
  • Notes, stickers and screenshots you save yourself, your concept maps, and your flashcard progress.
  • Offline study packs you download (concepts, definitions and drawings), until you delete them.
  • A short record of app crashes (the error and where it happened in the app's code, never screen text). It is sent only if you tap Settings → Send error report and choose where to send it.
  • If you sign in, your Google account's e-mail and name, shown in the app only. They are not sent anywhere.

What leaves your phone

  • Online AI (off until you turn it on): what you select for an answer, its text and its picture, is sent over an encrypted connection to the Exptra Ai server and the AI provider it uses, after a privacy filter. Bank, payment, password and OTP screens are never sent; card, Aadhaar, PAN and account numbers are hidden. The server does not keep the content.
  • Open sources: a short topic from your selection (a word or a name) may be looked up in Wikipedia, Wikidata or Wiktionary.
  • Pictures for notes ("Line art online", "Free pictures", study packs): the name of the concept (for example "photosynthesis") is searched in open picture libraries: Wikimedia Commons, Openverse, Pixabay, Openclipart, the Internet Archive, Wellcome Collection, NASA, the Cleveland Museum of Art, PubChem, PhyloPic and Iconify. Only the concept's name is sent, never your notes or screen. You can switch each library off in the picture picker (⚙ Sources). Nothing is looked up while Privacy mode is on.
  • Language packs and the handwriting reader are downloaded from Google when first needed.
  • Reports: an answer you report is sent for review, without the screen it was about.

Microphone

The microphone is used only after you tap the voice button or the Quick Settings tile, or hold the volume key (if you turned that on). Nothing listens by itself. Audio is not recorded or stored.

Sharing, selling, advertising

Exptra Ai shows no ads, sells no data, and shares nothing with anyone except as described above to answer what you asked.

Children

Exptra Ai is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Deleting your data

Clear the cache in Settings, delete notes and stickers in the app, sign out, or uninstall the app: everything it kept on the phone goes with it. The server keeps nothing to delete.
